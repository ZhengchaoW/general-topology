Components and local connectedness

Definition 7.25 Let XXX be a topological space. We define an equivalence relation on XXX by declaring x∼yx\sim yx∼y iff there exists a connected subspace of XXX containing both xxx and yyy. The equivalence classes of this relation are called the components of XXX.
